Scientific Heritage

Designed by an Industrial Chemist.

Many commercial paint brands are simply watered-down standard polymers mixed with dye. More Paint is different. Engineered directly by our **founder and Lead Industrial Chemist**, every batch undergoes rigorous chemical optimization.

Having worked on manufacturing floors for decades, our founder realized that standard paints fail rapidly in Nigeria because they cannot handle the persistent **95% relative humidity** and salt spray. He custom-engineered a molecular binder that cross-links under tropical heat, ensuring maximum adhesion on concrete, wood, and steel surfaces.
